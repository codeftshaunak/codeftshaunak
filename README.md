## 🎬 Building MirrorCut

**[MirrorCut](https://mirrorcut.ai)** is an AI video editor for creators and small brands who work from their own recordings.
Upload your footage, describe the edit, and its agent turns it into ads, Shorts and finished videos:

- ✂️ Cuts dead air and filler, and finds the best moments
- 💬 Captions, reframes for 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5, and adds motion graphics
- 🎞️ Every AI change lands on a real timeline, with Accept and Revert

Public launch: **November 2026**. [Try it →](https://mirrorcut.ai)
